RESIDENTIAL LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is executed on 2 February 2025 at Bengaluru, Karnataka.

BETWEEN

Mr. Harish Devaraj, aged 58 years, residing at 41, Second Cross, Malleshwaram, Bengaluru 560003 (hereinafter called the "Licensor", which expression includes his heirs, executors and assigns)

AND

Mr. Rohit Menon, aged 29 years, presently residing at 7/3 Hosur Road, Bengaluru 560029 (hereinafter called the "Licensee")

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of Flat No. 302, Second Floor, "Devaraj Nilaya", 18th Main, HSR Layout Sector 3, Bengaluru 560102, admeasuring approximately 1,050 sq. ft. (hereinafter the "Premises"), and has agreed to grant a licence to occupy the Premises on the terms below.

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 "Damage" means any mark, scratch, stain, crack, dent, discolouration, fixture loosening or other change to the condition of the Premises or its fittings from the condition recorded on the date of handover, other than such changes as the Licensor in his sole opinion considers to be ordinary wear and tear.

1.2 "Dues" means the Licence Fee, maintenance charges, electricity and water charges, interest, penalties and any other amount payable by the Licensee under this Agreement.

1.3 "Lock-in Period" means the first eleven (11) months of the Term.

2. TERM

2.1 The licence is granted for a period of eleven (11) months commencing 1 March 2025 and ending 31 January 2026 (the "Term").

2.2 The Term shall automatically renew for successive periods of eleven (11) months on the same terms unless the Licensee delivers written notice of non-renewal to the Licensor not less than one hundred and twenty (120) days before the expiry of the then-current Term. On each renewal the Licence Fee shall stand increased by twelve per cent (12%) over the Licence Fee payable in the preceding Term.

2.3 The Licensor may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving fifteen (15) days' written notice to the Licensee, without assigning any reason.

3. LICENCE FEE AND DEPOSIT

3.1 The Licensee shall pay a monthly Licence Fee of Rs. 38,000 (Rupees Thirty Eight Thousand only) in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month.

3.2 The Licensee shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 3,80,000 (Rupees Three Lakh Eighty Thousand only), equivalent to ten (10) months' Licence Fee, on or before the date of handover.

3.3 In the event of any delay in payment of the Licence Fee, the Licensee shall pay interest at the rate of three per cent (3%) per month on the outstanding amount, compounded monthly, in addition to a late fee of Rs. 2,500 per occurrence.

3.4 The Licensee shall additionally pay monthly maintenance charges of Rs. 4,500 to the housing society, and all electricity and water charges as per actuals. The Licensor may revise the maintenance charges payable by the Licensee from time to time to reflect amounts determined by the Licensor.

3.5 The security deposit shall be refunded to the Licensee after deduction of all Dues, the cost of rectifying all Damage, repainting charges, and any other amounts the Licensor deems appropriate. Such refund shall be made after the Licensor has completed his inspection and settlement of accounts.

4. LOCK-IN AND EARLY EXIT

4.1 Notwithstanding Clause 2.2, the Licensee shall not vacate the Premises during the Lock-in Period.

4.2 If the Licensee vacates the Premises before the expiry of the Lock-in Period, the Licensee shall be liable to pay the Licence Fee for the entire remaining part of the Lock-in Period, and the security deposit shall stand forfeited in full as pre-estimated damages.

4.3 If the Licensee wishes to vacate after the Lock-in Period, the Licensee shall give the Licensor three (3) months' prior written notice, and shall continue to pay the Licence Fee for the full notice period whether or not the Premises are occupied.

5. USE OF THE PREMISES

5.1 The Premises shall be used solely as a private residence by the Licensee and his immediate family. No other person shall be permitted to reside in or stay overnight at the Premises without the prior written permission of the Licensor.

5.2 The Licensee shall not keep pets, sublet, part with possession, or carry on any trade, business or professional activity, including work from home for any employer or client, at the Premises.

5.3 The Licensee shall not make any alteration, addition, or fixture to the Premises, including the installation of air-conditioning units, water purifiers, curtain rods or wall mounts, without the prior written consent of the Licensor. Any such installation shall become the property of the Licensor on termination.

5.4 The Licensor or his authorised representative may enter the Premises for inspection at any reasonable time. The Licensee shall not change or add any lock to the Premises and shall provide the Licensor with a duplicate key.

6. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

6.1 The Licensee shall maintain the Premises in good condition and shall be responsible for all repairs, whether minor or major, including repairs to plumbing, electrical wiring, sanitary fittings, water-proofing, geysers and any appliance provided by the Licensor, irrespective of the cause of the defect.

6.2 The Licensee shall, at his own cost, have the Premises painted and deep-cleaned before vacating, to the satisfaction of the Licensor.

6.3 The Licensor shall not be liable for any interruption in water supply, electricity, lift service, or any failure of common area facilities, and no abatement of the Licence Fee shall be claimed on that account.

7. SOCIETY RULES AND POLICIES

7.1 The Licensee shall comply with all rules, bye-laws and circulars of the housing society and with any additional house rules communicated by the Licensor, which may be amended by the Licensor from time to time at his sole discretion and which shall be binding on the Licensee from the date of communication.

8. DEFAULT AND REPOSSESSION

8.1 If the Licensee fails to pay any Dues within seven (7) days of the due date, or commits a breach of any term of this Agreement, the Licensor may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect.

8.2 Upon such termination, the Licensee shall vacate the Premises within forty-eight (48) hours, failing which the Licensee shall pay damages of Rs. 10,000 per day of continued occupation, and the Licensor shall be entitled to discontinue water and electricity supply to the Premises and to re-enter and take possession.

8.3 The Licensee shall not be entitled to claim any compensation for any belongings left in the Premises after such repossession, and the Licensor may dispose of such belongings without further notice.

9. INDEMNITY AND LIABILITY

9.1 The Licensee shall indemnify the Licensor against all claims, losses, damages, costs and expenses arising from the Licensee's occupation of the Premises or from any act or omission of the Licensee, his family, guests or visitors.

9.2 The Licensor shall not be liable for any loss, theft, injury or damage suffered by the Licensee or his belongings in the Premises or the building, from any cause whatsoever, including the Licensor's own negligence.

10. GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement constitutes a licence to occupy and does not create any tenancy, lease, or interest in the Premises in favour of the Licensee.

10.2 Any dispute shall be referred to the sole arbitration of an arbitrator nominated by the Licensor, with the seat of arbitration at Bengaluru. The arbitration costs shall be borne by the Licensee.

10.3 The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement and each renewal shall be borne solely by the Licensee.

10.4 This Agreement, together with the house rules referred to in Clause 7.1, represents the entire agreement between the Parties. No variation shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties, other than changes to the house rules and maintenance charges referred to in Clauses 7.1 and 3.4.

10.5 Time shall be of the essence in respect of all obligations of the Licensee under this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.

Licensor

Mr. Harish Devaraj

Licensee

Mr. Rohit Menon
